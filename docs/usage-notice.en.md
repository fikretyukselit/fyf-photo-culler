## Purpose and automated suggestions

FYF Photo Culler is a free, open-source desktop tool developed by volunteers of the Fikret Yuksel Foundation. It reads JPEG/JPG images in the folders you select, including subfolders, evaluates technical image quality and visual similarity, and suggests categories for your review. Scores and similarity groups are estimates, not a guarantee of photographic quality, uniqueness or suitability. You remain responsible for the final selection.

## Local processing and stored information

Photo analysis runs on your computer. This version does not send your photos, image metadata or selections to the Foundation, contributors or an AI service, and does not include analytics or advertising trackers. Its local processing service listens only on your computer; it is not an internet-hosted photo service.

The app creates local thumbnails and previews and saves session information, including file paths, analysis results and review decisions, so you can resume. These files may remain after closing the app. Protect access to your device and local storage; closing or uninstalling the app is not a promise that all cached or session data has been erased.

## Source files, export and backups

The normal analysis and export workflow reads source photos and copies categorized files to the destination shown before export. It does not intentionally move, delete or overwrite source photos. Rejected photos can also be copied into rejection folders. Exported copies can retain embedded metadata, including EXIF information; export does not anonymize images or remove location information.

Confirm your source and destination folders, available storage and results. Keep an independent backup before use. If you choose an iCloud, OneDrive, Dropbox, network or other synchronized location, the operating system or storage provider may transfer those files according to its own settings. Such transfers are outside this app’s control.

## Updates and external connections

After you accept this notice, the app automatically contacts GitHub to check for releases. Choosing to install an update downloads the update from GitHub and its delivery infrastructure. These requests expose ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to those providers and are subject to their policies. Photo content is not included in update requests. You can analyze and export photos without an internet connection; checking for and downloading updates requires one.
